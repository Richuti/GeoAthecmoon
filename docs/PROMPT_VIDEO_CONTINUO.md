# Video continuo para la portada

Una sola localización, un solo módulo y una cámara continua. Los textos y datos se añaden en la web, no en el video. Usar la duración máxima que permita la herramienta conservando la continuidad. Sin audio; composición horizontal con los sujetos principales cerca del centro para permitir recorte vertical.

```text
Create one continuous cinematic shot for an interactive lunar mission website.

SETTING AND CONTINUITY
The entire film takes place at ONE location: a rugged crater rim near the lunar south pole. Keep the exact same terrain, crater, robotic lander, materials and spatial relationships throughout. One uninterrupted camera movement. No cuts, scene changes, montage or teleportation. Do not morph the landscape or change the lander's design.

VISUAL JOURNEY
Begin with a wide, low view across the crater rim. The Sun is a SMALL distant disc grazing the horizon, only 1–2 degrees above it. Sunlight touches the highest ridges while the crater floor remains dark.

The camera slowly glides forward along the rim. In a subtle continuous time-lapse, the Sun moves sideways along the horizon and long shadows sweep across the SAME terrain. It never rises overhead.

Continue the camera movement with a gentle turn, revealing a small Earth hanging low above a distant ridge. Earth must remain physically plausible in size, approximately four times the Sun's apparent diameter, never an enormous planet filling the sky. Part of Earth disappears behind the ridge as the camera continues.

A compact generic robotic lunar lander enters the composition and descends slowly onto the nearby illuminated rim. Follow its descent without cutting. The lander has an octagonal body, gold and silver thermal insulation, four landing legs, one vertical solar panel and a small dish antenna. Any displaced dust travels close to the ground in ballistic paths and settles; no floating clouds.

After touchdown, the camera approaches slowly. Continue the time-lapse as the Sun slips behind the distant terrain. Its long shadow merges into darkness. Finish on the SAME lander in quiet lunar night, with very faint Earth illumination and a tiny, understated status light. Hold the final composition.

ART DIRECTION
Photorealistic lunar regolith, restrained cinematic composition, deep black sky, hard grazing sunlight, warm amber highlights and neutral grey terrain. Physically grounded, quiet and majestic. Smooth camera speed with no sudden acceleration.

Keep important subjects around the central area so the film can also be cropped vertically. Leave generous dark negative space for website text overlays. No text should be generated inside the video.

AVOID
No atmosphere, fog, clouds, floating dust, dense stars, giant Sun, giant Earth, blue sky, lens-flare streaks, neon effects, holograms, interface graphics, logos, flags, astronauts, recognizable spacecraft, captions or transitions.
```

El relato de la web acompañará cinco momentos del mismo video: Sol bajo → sombras y luz → contacto con la Tierra → elección del alunizaje → noche y batería. El video es una ilustración; las cifras de la interfaz siguen viniendo de los datos.
